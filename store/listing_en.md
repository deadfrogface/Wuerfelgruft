# Play Store listing (English draft)

**App name:** Dice Crypt (Würfelgruft)

**Short description (80 chars):**
Turn-based roguelike on a rotating cube. 100 floors, 10 worlds.

**Full description:**
A dungeon on a cube. Six sides, one move at a time, no time pressure. Rotate the cube, find the exit on another side and survive what moves only when you move.

Sometimes it is a key, sometimes switches on several sides, an offering altar, runes in the right order, a leader to defeat or a fight to outlast: every floor sets a different goal.

• 100 floors in 10 worlds, each with its own look, music, enemies, terrain and bosses
• Turn-based: no time pressure, every move counts, diagonals included
• 200+ enemies, each with its own look, attacks and movement, 20 bosses with rage phases and their own combat mechanics
• 64 upgrades, throwable items, oases and a merchant between worlds, plus loaded dice to gamble with
• 7 classes with strengths and weaknesses, 5 difficulty levels from "Easy" to "1 HP"
• Ascent levels and mutators for even harder runs
• 40+ skins to unlock, plus limited-time events with their own minigames and music: Halloween, Christmas, Easter, Valentine's Day, Italian and Hungarian festivals, a birthday and Vegas Night with blackjack, dice and roulette at the felt table
• 7 floor goals in rotation, enemies grow stronger with depth
• Daily run, login bonus, quests and achievements
• Permanent crypt stones to unlock upgrades at the forge
• Free to play, no account. Optional ads for bonus crypt stones, one-time ad-free purchase
• Saves and gameplay work offline

The game is available in English and German (follows the device language, switchable in Settings).
